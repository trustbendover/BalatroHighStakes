# Balatro High Stakes 0.7.8

- **Heat.** Clearing a blind fills the meter, in solo and with a friend. The meter is a slim bar on the right. It eases from yellow to red, and at 100 the flame runs both edges until Overdrive is spent.
- **How to play.** The first Heat Rush match explains the awards, in English and Dutch. Got it dismisses it. The meter can open it again.

**Install:** download the Installer zip, unzip it, run `Install.bat`. Already on 0.6.6 or newer? Use the in-game "Update available" button or `Update.bat`.
