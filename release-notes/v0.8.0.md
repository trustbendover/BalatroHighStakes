# Balatro High Stakes 0.8.0

- **Heat.** The pill sits on the right edge of the screen, clear of the consumables, the deck, and the jokers. The number, HEAT, and Best each stay on their own line under the pill.
- **Flame.** At 100 the meter uses Balatro's own flame, the same one behind a huge chips x mult score. The chips and mult flames are left alone.

**Install:** download the Installer zip, unzip it, run `Install.bat`. Already on 0.6.6 or newer? Use the in-game "Update available" button or `Update.bat`.
