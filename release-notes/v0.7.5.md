# Balatro High Stakes 0.7.5

- **Heat Rush.** A timed mode for one player, or two to eight. Clearing a blind fills Heat. At 100 the next hand is Overdrive, x1.5, and the meter empties. A fast clear can give a free reroll or $3. A boss beaten inside the clock can give a Tag. Going slow takes nothing away. Solo ends after ante 8. In a group, lives still eliminate you, and if the last players fall together the higher total Heat wins. Music: Heat Rush track by Lille Lilienthal
- **Spectate.** Follow a player and see their hand, jokers, and shop as view-only cards. Previous, next, back, rejoin, and stats stay.
- **Chaos.** Each Chaos Spin and twist shows its name and a one-line description, on a banner and in the chat.
- **Lives.** The number the host picks, from 1 to 16, is what the match starts with. Gambel Time uses that number too, up to 16.
- **Menu.** A saved match is an offer: Rejoin or a new lobby. Create and Join clear the old match. Money and other run actions wait until a run and its HUD exist, so the main menu no longer crashes on a stale rejoin.

**Install:** download the Installer zip, unzip it, run `Install.bat`. Already on 0.6.6 or newer? Use the in-game "Update available" button or `Update.bat`.
