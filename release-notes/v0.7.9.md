# Balatro High Stakes 0.7.9

- **Heat.** The number, HEAT, and Best each stay on one line under the pill. HEAT no longer wraps, and the number no longer sits on the word.
- **Meter.** The pill sits further right and further down, so the vanilla consumable counter (0/2) stays clear of the 50 tick.
- **Spectate.** The watched hand, jokers, shop, and blind update as that player plays, discards, buys, and picks a new blind. The cards stay view-only.

**Install:** download the Installer zip, unzip it, run `Install.bat`. Already on 0.6.6 or newer? Use the in-game "Update available" button or `Update.bat`.
