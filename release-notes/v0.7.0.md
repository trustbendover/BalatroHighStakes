# Balatro High Stakes 0.7.0

- **Gambel Time.** A party mode for 1 to 8 players. Alone you face The House. With friends, every boss is a fight and the host's Chaos Spin changes the stakes. The shop has Double or Nothing and the Gambel Wheel, once per ante. The original track "GAMBEL TIME" by Bass Gas M plays during the run, used with permission.
- **Chat.** In the lobby and during a match: a top-right dropdown, coloured player names, a 550-character limit, and a scrollable history.
- **Uneven teams.** 5 players are 3v2 and 7 are 4v3. The smaller side's score is scaled up to the larger side.
- **Who is here, and how it ended.** Player rows show connected, waiting to rejoin, rejoining, watching, or gone. The end of the match has places, damage, and awards.
- **High Stakes Multiplayer.** That is the ruleset name. Each mode says how you win. A public lobby card shows the host's match settings.
- **Steamodded.** The installer still pins Steamodded 1.0.0~BETA-1620a and Lovely 0.10.0. The mod also runs on Steamodded 26.829 and 26.1002.0.

**Verified:** the Lua test suite, the TypeScript reference server, and those same scenarios on the in-game Lua host. Live on Windows Balatro: Gambel Time, chat, Double or Nothing, the Gambel Wheel, and the shop layout at 1440p.

**Install:** download the Installer zip, unzip it, run `Install.bat`. Already on 0.6.6 or newer? Use the in-game "Update available" button or `Update.bat`.
