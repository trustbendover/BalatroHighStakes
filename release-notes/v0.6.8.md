**Balatro High Stakes** is an unofficial fork of Balatro Multiplayer by Trust Ben Dover. Credits to Virtualized and the Balatro Multiplayer team (GPL-3.0). It is not affiliated with LocalThunk, Playstack or the Balatro Multiplayer team.

## What's new in 0.6.8
- **Host migration.** If the host leaves, quits or loses connection (lobby, shop or mid-PvP), the next player becomes host and the match goes on for everyone. A frozen host is detected in about 10 seconds.
- **Rejoin keeps your seat.** A player who drops keeps their seat for the rejoin window and comes back with the same player id and standings.
- **Spectators.** Watch a running match with live standings.
- **Start waits for Ready.** The host can only start when every guest has pressed Ready.
- **Lobby presets.** Four built-in presets plus four own slots.
- **Patch notes pop-up** with the new Balatro High Stakes logo, and match stats at the end.
- **Steam only.** Plays over Steam peer-to-peer only; an old server setting in `.env` is switched to Steam once (backup kept).
- **New GitHub name: `trustbendover/BalatroHighStakes`.** Old links and older versions still find updates through GitHub's redirect.
- **Installer:** warns about and backs up the official Balatro Multiplayer mods (they clash), and can optionally download All-in-Jest 0.7.2 (default No). All-in-Jest 0.7.4 needs a newer Steamodded and is skipped with a clear notice.
- **Bug fixes:** simultaneous last-life losses, split-brain hosts, rejoin after timeouts, `update_check=false` / `compat_check=false` honoured again, cleaner logs, panels with fixed widths.

## Important
- **Everyone (host and friends) must be on 0.6.8.**
- **Already on 0.6.6 or 0.6.7?** Click **Update available** in the main menu, or run `Update.bat`. Your settings are kept.
- **New install:** download the Installer zip, unzip it and run `Install.bat`.
