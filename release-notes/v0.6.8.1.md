## What's new in 0.6.8.1 (hotfix)
- **Leave keeps your seat.** Leaving a running match through the menu now holds your seat exactly like a disconnect: rejoin within the rejoin window (lobby code, Steam or the Rejoin button).
- **Watch match from the menu.** Player names show up instantly, Prev/Next follows anyone, and while your seat is held a **Rejoin (m:ss)** button takes it back.
- **Waiting-to-rejoin banner** clears when the player is back; when their time runs out it shows **"X left"**.
- **Anti-abuse:** time keeps running while you are away (you rejoin at the match's current ante), and a seat that is away when a PvP resolves counts as the lowest score and **loses a life** (not in Teams).
