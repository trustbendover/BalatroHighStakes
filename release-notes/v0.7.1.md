# Balatro High Stakes 0.7.1

A small hotfix on top of 0.7.0.

- **Public lobbies.** The columns line up. Join stays inside the panel. Each lobby's settings sit under its own row. There is one Back button. Long host names are shortened.
- **Rejoin.** The same player is listed once. The name no longer runs into "Connected".
- **Blind select.** Picking a blind no longer crashes if that screen is already gone.
- **PvP.** A player who left and is waiting to rejoin no longer freezes the blind for everyone else.
- **Gambel Wheel.** Your prize waits until your own wheel stops.
- **Shop odds.** Double or Nothing wins about 18 times in 37. The wheel still has big prizes, and the small losses come up a bit more often.

**Install:** download the Installer zip, unzip it, run `Install.bat`. Already on 0.6.6 or newer? Use the in-game "Update available" button or `Update.bat`.
